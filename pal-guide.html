🧬 Breeding — Your Clear Path

Start here

Follow the steps in order. Steps 1–4 get you breeding today; step 5 is where the real power comes from.

⚠️ Read this first — the golden rule of Palworld breeding.
There are no fixed "recipes" for most pals. Every pal has a hidden rank number, and the baby is the species closest to the average of the two parents. Same parents = always the same baby.

Formula: child rank = (parent A + parent B + 1) ÷ 2

This is why Penking + Bushi no longer gives Anubis. It worked in early access because the average landed on Anubis's old rank of 570. In the 1.0 update, 72 new pals were added and Anubis's rank moved to 480 — so that pair now lands on Sibelyx instead. Every guide still listing the old combo is recycled early-access text.

3
Breed your first eggThe mechanical loop
  • Put one male + one female into the farm — drag them in from the Palbox, or pick a pal up and throw it into the pen. Check the ♂/♀ icon on the pal's summary screen.
  • Put cake INTO the farm's own inventory (open the farm and deposit) — not into a chest nearby.
  • Wait a few minutes → egg appears. Pick it up.
  • Drop the egg in an Incubator and match its temperature: campfire/heater for scorching eggs, cooler for frozen ones. Wrong temperature = much slower.
  • Babies hatch at level 1 no matter how strong the parents were. That's normal — they catch up fast.
4
Pick your projectTwo very different goals — know which one you're doing
🅰️ Passive farmingBreed a pal with ITSELF (Bushi × Bushi). Species is guaranteed, so every egg is a roll for better passive skills. This is where power actually comes from. Start here.
🅱️ Species huntingMixing two different species to create a third. Fun, but you must look the pair up first — see step 6.
5
The 2+2 passive strategy⭐ The single most valuable technique in the game

Each baby rolls up to 4 passive slots: roughly 24% from dad, 20% from mum, 21% a random new one, 34% empty. So: get the 4 passives you want across the parents — 2+2, 1+3 or 0+4 splits all work — then spam eggs until one baby inherits all four. Key rule (community-tested in 1.0): keep the parents' combined desired passives at exactly 4 and nothing else — junk passives crowd out your targets. Volume beats perfection.

6
Looking up a species combo (do this before spending cakes)Because the combo websites are unreliable
  • Open paldb.cc → your pal's page → "Breed 2 Pals" or "Parent Calculator". paldb is datamined from the actual game files — trust it over guide articles.
  • Sanity-check any suggestion with the formula. Known ranks: Bushi 640 · Penking 520 · Anubis 480.
  • To hit a target, the two parents' ranks must sum to roughly target × 2 − 1. For Anubis (480) that's a sum of ~959.
  • Most 1.0 "breeding combo" sites contradict each other and invent pal names. Ignore them.
🏺 Still want Anubis? Skip the roulette — there's a guaranteed one in the world: the alpha "Guardian of the Dark Sun", Level 55, in the Anubis/Twilight Dunes at −134, −94. Too strong for you at 34; come back around Lv 45–50 and catch it. Worth it: 1.0 buffed Anubis to Handiwork 6 · Mining 6 · Transporting 4.

1.0 specialty cakes — which one to actually use

🥬 Best value for your passive line: Vegetable Cake. Two eggs per breeding cycle, each with an independent passive roll — it literally doubles your 2+2 odds per timer. Unlock it as soon as it appears in your tech tree. Save Special Cake for when both parents already carry the right passives; ignore Deluxe Veg (mutations) until endgame.

Base split that works

Keep pals working

😵 Low sanity → slacking & illness. Build 2–3 Hot Springs, give everyone a bed, cook real food (salad beats raw berries).
🏥 Depressed / Weakened need High Grade Medical Supplies (5 Ingot + 5 Horn + 2 Bone, Medieval Medicine Workbench). Cold/Sprain = Low Grade. Ulcer/Fracture = standard.
🚧 Stuck pals = uneven terrain. Build on foundations, wide ramps, no narrow doorways. Fix by picking up & re-throwing, or Palbox retrieve/redeploy.

💡 Golden Rules

Golden rules

1️⃣ Catch 5 of everything. Capture bonus XP is the whole levelling game.
2️⃣ Weight → HP. Never Attack, never Work Speed.
3️⃣ Condense your workers, not just fighters — in 1.0 it raises work suitability levels.
4️⃣ Pal Souls aren't clutter — Statue of Power, up to +60% per stat.
5️⃣ Every effigy, every fast-travel statue. The detour always pays.
6️⃣ Passives > species. A 4-passive common pal beats a bare legendary at base work.
7️⃣ Trust datamined sources (paldb.cc) over guide articles — most "1.0 updated" combo sites are auto-generated and wrong.
